# Paper Title Suggestions

## Research Context
- **Main Contribution**: Applying Two-Phase Verification (Wu et al. 2024) to multi-agent medical diagnosis systems
- **Key Innovation**: Multi-agent consultation with self-verification and uncertainty quantification
- **Domain**: Medical question answering (Respiratory, Cardiology, Neurology)
- **Focus**: Showing Multi-Agent + Two-Phase Verification outperforms alternatives

---

## Title Options (Ranked by Recommendation)

### Option 1: **Multi-Agent Focus** (RECOMMENDED)
**"Multi-Agent Medical Diagnosis with Two-Phase Verification: Improving Accuracy and Uncertainty Quantification"**

**Why**: 
- Highlights the main contribution (multi-agent + verification)
- Clear and descriptive
- Emphasizes both accuracy and uncertainty (key metrics)
- Academic but accessible

**Variations**:
- "Multi-Agent Medical Diagnosis Enhanced by Two-Phase Verification: A Framework for Improved Accuracy and Uncertainty Quantification"
- "Enhancing Multi-Agent Medical Diagnosis through Two-Phase Verification and Uncertainty Quantification"
