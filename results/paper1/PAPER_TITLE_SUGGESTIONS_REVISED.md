# Paper Title Suggestions (Revised - Based on Actual Results)

## Critical Issue Identified

**Problem**: The original title suggestions claimed "improving accuracy" or "outperforming" other configurations, but the results show:
- **Single Specialist + Two-Phase**: 70.0% accuracy, ECE 0.158, AUROC 0.611 (BEST)
- **Multi-Agent + Two-Phase**: 63.3% accuracy, ECE 0.568, AUROC 0.545

**Multi-Agent + Two-Phase does NOT outperform Single Specialist + Two-Phase.**

## What the Paper Actually Shows

1. ✅ **Two-Phase Verification helps both single and multi-agent** (improves calibration/discrimination)
2. ✅ **Novel application** of Two-Phase Verification to multi-agent systems
3. ✅ **Framework contribution** (how to apply verification to multi-agent)
4. ⚠️ **Multi-agent doesn't necessarily outperform single** (but verification helps both)
